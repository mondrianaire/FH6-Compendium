# Upgrades since the last packet — for another design pass

**Date:** 2026-09-11 · **Code reviewed last time:** v52 · **Code now:** panel v192 · live v108 · app v101 · styles v151

The `src/` snapshot has been refreshed to the shipped code, so it now matches what these notes
describe. This document is the **delta**: what changed since your last review, why, and the specific
calls we want a second opinion on. The hard constraints in `README.md` still hold — judge everything at
**1080 × 1751 portrait, zero scrollbars, the game's own design language.** Where a decision below fights
one of those constraints, say so.

The bulk of the work landed in **Course mode** (`MODE.suggest === "course"`: you're in a timed
event / Rivals / a marked reference loop). That mode now has a genuine two-pane anatomy and a deep
single-turn drill-down that did not exist at v52. Most of what follows lives there.

---

## 1. Course mode is now a real two-pane layout

**Left pane = the whole-course map. Right pane = everything else, tabbed.** At v52 the left pane
carried the per-turn detail; that was wrong — the per-turn map belongs beside its own stats. Now:

- **LEFT (always):** the course hero, the whole-course map, and the sortable turn list — the course as
  a whole, never changing out from under you.
- **RIGHT (tabbed):** `Current lap · Turn analysis · Corners · Conclusions · Build`. Selecting a turn
  anywhere jumps the right pane to **Turn analysis** for that turn.

**Judge:** is the left/right split legible at 1080-wide portrait, or does the right pane's tab bar
compete with the left pane's own controls? Should the hero live above the map (current) or span both panes?

## 2. The course hero (above the left map) — finalized

A slim identity band above the map: the route **glyph** (mini SVG of the path, green start dot / pink
finish dot) with a start/finish/**loop-or-point-to-point** legend, then a stat line —
**length · median lap · best lap · climb · turns measured.** This replaced a bare title.

**Judge:** the glyph + legend + stat line is a lot in a short band. Is the hierarchy right (what should
be biggest?), and does the glyph earn its space next to the full map right below it?

## 3. Single-turn drill-down (right pane, "Turn analysis") — new, and the centre of gravity

Selecting a turn opens, in order:

1. **The corner map** — the single corner zoomed to fill the width: the road, a translucent 5-phase
   band underlay (braking / turn-in / mid / exit / straight, in the WHERE palette), the apex ringed and
   labelled, a travel chevron, entry/apex/exit speed pills, and **every driven pass's line through the
   corner.** Default colouring: **each trace one solid colour by its pace position** (green = fastest
   through the corner → red = slowest). A legend toggle switches to the old **point-by-point speed
   gradient** as a filter. (This is the newest change — see §7.)
2. **Diagnosis card** — the turn's dominant error, or "nothing to change here."
3. **Where the time goes** — headline turn-time, a per-phase time-budget bar, and a typical-vs-best
   table with a gap visualiser.
4. **Fastest passes (leaderboard)** — every pass ranked fastest-first; each row is lap time, car
   (class pill + short name), turn seconds, and a per-phase apex-mph cell coloured by grip. **Click a
   row to isolate that lap** (see §6).
5. **Grip through the turn** — one distribution bar (calm / understeer / oversteer / drift / impact)
   with the share of samples, never a single lossy swatch.

**Judge:** five stacked sections in one pane at portrait height. What's the right order, what collapses
when empty, and is anything redundant (e.g. the time-budget bar vs the leaderboard's turn-s column)?

## 4. Detected-error cards — frequency / severity / tuning

Each error names its **phase + grip state**, a **frequency %** (share of same-kind corners showing the
same error), a **severity 0–100** (lap-time impact × error type × amount), and a **tuning fix** (the
analyzer's own detector rule where one fired, else a phase+axle rule). In a **spec event** (see §8) the
tuning line becomes a **driving cue** instead, because the car/tune are fixed.

**Judge:** three metrics + a fix per card is dense. Which of freq / severity / amount earns the big
number, and how should severity read at a glance (colour, bar, both)?

## 5. Inline identity bar + status bar consolidation

- A slim **inline car-identity bar** (`#idbar`) now sits with the header band (we kept both,
  deliberately).
- The **Services** controls (start/stop/restart the three lab processes) moved OFF a tab and into a
  hover dropdown in the top status bar.
- **All alert/ticker surfaces were removed.** `#alerts` now carries only the save-picker when one is
  genuinely needed, and **collapses to zero height** otherwise (it used to reserve ~96px of empty band).

**Judge:** did removing the alert band leave the header doing too much? Is the identity bar redundant
with the header's own car block?

## 6. Lap isolation across both maps

Clicking a **Fastest-passes** row dims every other lap's trace and lifts the selected lap — on **both**
the single-corner map (right) and the whole-course map (left) at once. Click again to clear.

**Judge:** is a two-map simultaneous highlight legible, or does the left-map isolation compete for
attention while you're reading the right-pane stats?

## 7. Corner-map trace colouring — default changed (newest)

Previously every line was coloured **point-by-point by speed** (red slow → green fast within the
corner). That's now a **toggle** ("speed"); the **default** ("position") paints **each trace one solid
colour by its pace rank through the corner** — green fastest → red slowest, the same green→amber→red the
course map's lap-time gradient uses. Rationale: with many overlaid laps, a per-point gradient is a wash;
a solid pace colour lets you see at a glance which lines are the quick laps.

**Judge:** solid-by-rank vs speed-gradient as the default — right call? With ~30 overlaid traces, does a
30-step green→red ramp read, or should it bucket (e.g. quartiles / top-3 highlighted)?

## 8. New "Current lap" live tab + spec-event recognition

- **Current lap** (leads the right pane while you're driving a course lap): one row per turn, appearing
  the instant you finish it — a **speed rating vs your own history** for that turn (`★ best yet` /
  `Nth of N`, plus mph off your best apex) and the **grip loss** (axle + phase, or clean).
- **Spec events:** some Rivals hand you a fixed, temporary car + tune. The dashboard now recognises this
  and short-circuits tuning advice (tuning is moot) to **driving cues** and a "◈ SPEC CAR" state.

**Judge:** the live tab is a fourth thing competing to lead the right pane (with Turn analysis, Corners,
Build). What should win the pane while actively driving?

## 9. Course map polish

Lap-time **gradient colouring** (each lap's line green fastest → red slowest across the drawn set), a
**floating collapsible legend pill** (bottom-left, so it never covers turn numbers), a **turn-phase
view** toggle (paint the whole road by the 5-phase model), legible **turn numbers** with a dark halo,
and a **course browser** with sorting (name / laps / length / kind) + a temporary course-browser mode
(double-click a map in free roam).

**Judge:** the map now has two view toggles (lap-time / phases) in a floating pill plus the shared data
filter bar elsewhere. Is the control surface for the map coherent, or scattered?

---

## Not visual, but it changes what you're judging: the data behind it

The corner analysis used to run on **one lap** — the export kept only whole, clean, non-rewound laps, and
on a practice-heavy course that was a single lap out of thirty. It now includes **every driven pass**
(~30 laps/turn). So the leaderboard, grip distributions and error frequencies you're reviewing are now
populated with real spread — **the screens you judge should show full tables and real distributions, not
one-row placeholders.** If a screen still looks empty, that's a capture/state issue, not the design.

## The question for this pass

At v52 you told us the anatomy; we built it. Now judge the **filled** surface at **1080 × 1751**:

1. **Course mode's right pane** carries up to five stacked sections and a five-tab bar. What is the right
   vertical budget per section, what collapses, and what should never be more than one tap away?
2. **The single-turn view** is now the product's centre of gravity. Does the corner map + leaderboard +
   diagnosis read as one coherent story, or three tools stacked?
3. **Two new colour languages** landed (pace-rank on the corner map, lap-time gradient on the course
   map) on top of the existing WHERE-palette phase bands and the grip palette. Is that too many colour
   systems on one screen? Which should yield?
4. Anything here that **displays because we can**, not because it changes the next decision — name it to cut.
