# Mid-project verification pass — 2026-06-08

Three independent research agents cross-checked every v1/v2 claim against
**forums** (forums.forza.net, GTPlanet) and **independent databases** (game8,
Forza Fandom, kudosprime, CODMunity), explicitly weighting for source
independence and hunting for contradictions.

## Headline

The **biggest finding is methodological**: most of the public "FH6 guide" web is a
tight cluster of SEO/AI-generated tier-lists that copy each other almost verbatim
(idioms like "rarely wasted PI" and "menace in both Touge and street" recur word-
for-word). That is *not* independent corroboration. When we filtered to genuinely
independent sources:

- **Qualitative claims held up well** — AWD-swap meta, grip/brakes/weight before
  power, the variable set, starter cars, the free GT-R NISMO journal path, the
  existence of the skill-point and AFK farms.
- **Several specific NUMBERS were wrong or fabricated** — corrected below.

## Confirmed (✅ multiple independent sources)
- Tuning variable set matches prior Forza titles. (ForzaTune, ForzaFire)
- Grip/brakes/weight first, power last; weight "rarely wasted PI" (direction). (GTPlanet + guides)
- Heavy engine swaps suit power tracks, hurt touge. (GTPlanet users, guides)
- Starter cars: Nissan Silvia, GMC Jimmy, Toyota Celica GT-Four — you get all three. (GameRant, TechTimes, OperationSports)
- GT-R NISMO free via Touge & Street Rivals Collection Journal (Tier 3). (game8, gamerguides)
- WRX STI '04 = 30,000 cr. (game8, Fandom, kudosprime)
- AMG One = top S2; Gemera = best/near-best S2 all-rounder. (dexerto, dotesports)
- BRZ FE = ~450k at Sotoyama Ski Resort; F-450 = best D–B cross-country. (game8, Fandom)
- Skill-point EventLab codes 861224889 & 401393298 exist. (VGC, keengamer)
- Daily-wheelspin house concept; Festival Playlist replaces Forzathon. (multiple)

## Downgraded to ⚠️ contested / 🟡 probable (sources disagree or single-origin)
- **Assist multiplier %s** (+15/+15/+10/+10=50%): single self-copying SEO chain, no
  primary backing. Forza historically uses one aggregate difficulty modifier. AI
  difficulty is the real, established lever. → CONTESTED.
- **Diff baseline 55%/15%**: ForzaFire says 100%/45%, grindout says 50-70%/10-15%.
  → CONTESTED; road-tune from scratch.
- **"30/70 power split"** as a distinct spec: it's the diff CENTER BALANCE (~70%
  rear), not a separate slider. → clarified.
- **22B "best A-class rally"**: single-origin SEO; no community crown. → probable.
- **Tune order springs-vs-alignment**: ForzaTune reverses them. → noted.
- **Revuelto 39-SP / 10-SP-per-21s**: single-source, plausible. → flagged.
